About BuzzerBlock

BuzzerBlock shows sports schedules, standings and scores without spoilers. Scores stay hidden until you choose to show them.

Your data

There is no account and no sign in. Your favorite teams and settings stay on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
