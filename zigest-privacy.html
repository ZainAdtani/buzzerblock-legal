Zigest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version

Zigest collects no data. There is no account, no sign in, and no Zigest server. Your text, digests, and Library stay on your device.

If you paste a YouTube link, your phone contacts YouTube directly to read the video's title and captions. YouTube receives the link you chose, like any web browser would. We never see it.

Who we are

Zigest is made by Adtani Education Ventures LLC. Questions: zkadtani@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • The text you paste or type
  • Your digests, Triage lists, and Library
  • YouTube links you share to Zigest
  • Your saved drafts

All of this is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

YouTube links

This only happens when you paste or share a YouTube link and ask Zigest to read it. Your phone sends the request straight to YouTube, with cookies turned off. The request does not go through any Zigest server, because we do not have one. YouTube's own privacy policy covers what YouTube does with it.

If you use the Share button in the YouTube or Safari share sheet and pick Zigest, only the link is saved. It is saved on your device in a space shared between Zigest and its share button. It is never sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, email, or password
  • No network requests to us. The only request the app makes is to YouTube, and only when you ask it to read a YouTube link
  • No ads and no ad tracking
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools
  • No location access
  • We do not sell or share your information, because we do not have any

Children

Zigest collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top first.
