Zigest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The short version

Zigest collects no data. There is no account and no sign in. Nothing you paste or type ever leaves your device.

Who we are

Zigest is made by Adtani Education Ventures LLC. Questions: zkadtani@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • The text you paste or type
  • Your digests and Triage lists
  • Your saved drafts

All of this is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, email, or password
  • No network requests that carry your content
  • No ads and no ad tracking
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools
  • No location access
  • We do not sell or share your information, because we do not have any

Children

Zigest collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top first.
