BuzzerBlock Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version

BuzzerBlock shows sports schedules, standings, and scores without spoilers. There is no account, no sign in, no ads, and no tracking. Your favorite teams and settings stay on your iPhone. We do not collect your personal information.

Who we are

BuzzerBlock is made by Adtani Education Ventures LLC. Questions: zkadtani@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your favorite teams
  • Your settings, like Show Scores
  • A saved copy of schedules and team info so your widgets can show it

This is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network requests

Public sports data. The app and its widgets download public schedules, scores, standings, player info, and team logos from ESPN's public sports feeds. These requests do not include your name or your list of teams, and there is no account to attach them to. Like any website, those servers can see basic technical details such as your IP address. ESPN's own privacy policy applies to its servers.

App updates. The app checks Expo's update service for small app fixes. That check sends technical details like the app version and platform, not personal information.

Purchases

BuzzerBlock Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card, your name, or your Apple account. The app only asks Apple whether Pro is unlocked on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules tip-off reminders for your favorite teams right on your iPhone. They never include scores. No notification data is sent to us.

Sharing

If you tap Text, Email, or Copy link, your iPhone opens Messages or Mail, or copies a link. You choose who gets it. We do not see it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, email, or password
  • No ads and no ad tracking
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools
  • No location access
  • We do not sell or share your information

Children

BuzzerBlock is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change what BuzzerBlock collects, we will update this page and the date at the top first.
